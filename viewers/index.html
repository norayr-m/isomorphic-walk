Isomorphic Walk — 3D viewers

Three web-based NGL structural viewers, one per claim. Open in Chrome. Requires internet (loads PDB entries from RCSB). Amateur engineering project, no competitive claims.

1 · Asymptotic scaling

HIV-1 capsid · 313 236 atoms · 6 ms

Swift BFS traverses the full HIV-1 capsid contact graph in 6 milliseconds on a laptop. ProDy GNM's dense Kirchhoff matrix at this size needs 784 GB — mathematical exclusion, not a speed contest.

2 · Allosteric pathway

PTP1B 1T49 · ΔF1 = +0.113

BFS suboptimal tube vs Euclidean cylinder on pre-registered seeds Cys215→Leu192. The nine residues the graph finds that a straight line misses are visible in green. DeepThink ESTABLISH verdict on the parent cohort (median ΔF1 +0.071, 86% wins).

3 · IsoWalk v5 bidirectional

4AKE · coverage fixed, signal not

Variant-5 bidirectional pilot on adenylate kinase. Toggle between first_flip_A, first_flip_B, meet-in-middle, and biological displacement. Architecture doubled coverage but the flip-order doesn't track biology — open question on item 4.
